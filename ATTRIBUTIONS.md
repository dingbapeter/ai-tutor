# Attributions

This platform stands on open work, and we credit it with joy. The canonical,
user-visible list lives in `config/credits.json` (served at `/credits` and
linked from the app). Rules for adding anything new: `docs/RESEARCH.md` →
licensing rulebook. Never adopt a non-commercial-licensed project; where a
license forbids extraction we use the project's API instead and credit it here
all the same.

Curriculum packs that adapt openly-licensed content (CC BY) must carry the
source and license inside the pack's `attribution` field, displayed in-app
wherever that content is taught.
