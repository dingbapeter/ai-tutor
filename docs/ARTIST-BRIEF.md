# Job post: 13 realistic tutor characters for a children's learning app

Paste this into Fiverr, Upwork or a direct message to a 3D artist. The
checker at dingba.ai/studio decides acceptance, so nobody has to argue
about whether a delivery is right.

---

**Title:** Realistic 3D head-and-shoulders characters from portraits, with
ARKit face blendshapes, delivered as glTF (.glb)

**What I need**

Thirteen realistic human characters, each matching a portrait I will send
(front view; I can send a second angle on request). Head and shoulders
only. They will be shown in a web browser on phones and must speak with
lip sync, smile, blink and look around, so the face must carry the
standard 52 ARKit expression blendshapes.

MetaHuman Creator is the preferred tool. Epic's licence allows MetaHuman
characters to be exported and used in any engine, so a MetaHuman export is
welcome. Character Creator, Daz or hand-sculpted work is also fine if it
meets the checklist below.

**Delivery, per character**

One file, `name.glb` (glTF 2.0 binary), that passes the free checker at
https://dingba.ai/studio. Drag your file onto that page; it runs in your
browser, uploads nothing, and lists anything wrong in plain words. A
delivery is accepted when the page says "Ready: this character meets the
contract". The checklist it enforces:

- all 52 ARKit blendshapes, named exactly (jawOpen, mouthSmileLeft, and so
  on), every one of them really moving the face; a blendshape with the
  right name that moves nothing is a fail;
- bones named Hips, Spine, Neck, Head, LeftEye, RightEye, with both eye
  bones parented under Head, and the head mesh skinned to them;
- 30,000 to 60,000 triangles;
- textures embedded in the file, 2048 px or smaller, under 15 MB total;
- built in metres (a bust about 0.6 m tall), facing +Z with +Y up, in a
  neutral pose with every blendshape at zero.

Also send the source files (Unreal, Blender or Character Creator project)
so the character can be revised later.

**Rights**

Full commercial rights to the delivered characters assigned to me, for
use in a commercial product worldwide. No third-party paid assets unless
the licence allows redistribution in a product, and you tell me which
ones. No scanned or copied likeness of a real person other than the
portraits I supply, which are my own.

**Payment**

Per character, on the Studio saying "Ready". Please quote a price per
character and an estimated turnaround for the first one; the remaining
twelve follow once the first is accepted. The first one is the test of
the whole pipeline, so expect one round of notes on it.

**Questions welcome before you quote**, especially about the export step:
MetaHuman faces are joint-driven, so the 52 blendshapes have to be baked
on export (Holotype's MetaHuman-to-glTF plugin on Fab does this, as does
BlendShapeExporterV2). If you have done this before, say so; it is the
step most people trip on.
